About the newsroom

Independent financial journalism, built for clarity.

Investment Banking is an independent online publication devoted to evidence-based financial and business journalism. We explain the institutions, markets and ideas shaping the financial world with accuracy, context and accountability.

Publication brief

Coverage
Markets, banking, business, investigations, ESG and FinTech
Newsroom
A distributed editorial team based in the United States
Standard
Evidence before speed; context before noise

Our mission

Reporting that helps readers understand what matters—and why.

Our purpose is to equip readers with clear, verifiable coverage of the monetary, business and technological forces influencing today’s world. We value precision over haste and explanation over spectacle.

We report for finance professionals, business leaders, policymakers, students and readers who want a sharper understanding of how money and institutions influence everyday life.

What guides us

Four principles shape every story.

01

Accuracy first

We verify material facts and favour factual precision over being first to publish.

02

Useful context

We show readers the sources, history and surrounding facts needed to understand a development.

03

Editorial independence

Coverage decisions remain free from corporate, partisan and government influence.

04

Open accountability

When an error is identified, we correct it clearly and promptly under published standards.

Who we are

A distributed newsroom with one editorial standard.

Our newsroom brings together journalists, editors, analysts and investigators with experience across digital and traditional media. Working across locations helps us follow stories between sectors while applying consistent editorial oversight.

Every report is reviewed before publication. Commentary is clearly identified and kept separate from reported news. We do not accept compensation to advocate for—or suppress—editorial coverage.
